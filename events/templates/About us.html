Our Mission

Where Is My Event.com was founded with a simple goal: to make finding local and international events as easy as a single click. From small community gatherings in Vadodara to massive concerts in London, we bring everything under one roof.

We believe that life is better when people come together. Our platform empowers event organizers to reach thousands of potential attendees without the hassle of high intermediate commissions.
